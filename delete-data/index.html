Delete your account and data

Delete directly in the game

  1. Open Settings → Delete my data.
  2. Read the notice and confirm both deletion prompts.

An internet connection is required.

Alternatively: request deletion by email

You can also request account and data deletion for Chef Sort: Restaurant Rush by BrainBlooms by email.

Request deletion by emailbrainblooms.support@gmail.com

Email subject: “Daten löschen” (delete data).

Required information

Please include one of the following:

  • Ten-digit player ID: find it under Settings and tap to copy.
  • Google Play order number for a purchase: starts with GPA. and appears in your purchase confirmation.

You can contact us without these details. However, we need one of them to locate and delete your account; your email address alone is not enough.

Further information

See our privacy policy for the scope of deletion, exceptions and timeframes.
